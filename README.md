`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes one original dad joke to `/output/entry.txt` — 157 characters, inside the gate's 1–200 range. Deterministic: the same line, byte for byte, on every run. The joke: a kid asks his dad what his superpower is; the dad says he can turn any normal conversation into a pun in under five seconds; the kid says "Prove it" — and the dad's answer proves it on the spot.
